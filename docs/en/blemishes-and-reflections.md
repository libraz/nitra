# Blemishes and reflections

This page covers filling a blemish and blurring a reflection, and the limits of the latter.

## Filling a blemish

A blemish is filled where it is clicked. Skin from around the mark is copied in rather than smoothed over, so the pores come with it.

## Blurring a reflection

A bystander caught in a reflection (a corneal catchlight, a mirror, metal or glass) is blurred inside a circle that is placed on the picture. It is a blur filter run in a selection: the average reads the photograph around the circle so that the edge does not show, and nothing outside the ring changes.

The strength is a fraction of each circle's own radius, so one setting suits an eye and a mirror at once. One click on a detected iris places the circle at its width.

## Limits

- **Blurring a reflection is not erasure.** nitra makes no claim about what could be recovered from the file. How much detail is left is what the slider says and nothing more; this is not removal in the ISO/IEC 27038 sense. The result is judged by looking at it.
- **At the top of the slider an eye loses its catchlight.** A reach wider than the circle flattens it to close to one tone, and nothing is drawn back in to replace it.
